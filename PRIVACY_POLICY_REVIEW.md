## Suggested app-specific wording for the revised notice

### Client accounts, bookings and correspondence

We use your name, email address, contact and billing details to manage your
account, send email verification codes, handle enquiries, process booking
requests, and communicate booking and payment updates. Bookings and waivers
are shown in the client portal after verification of the corresponding email.
The portal uses session cookies for sign-in and security. Choosing to stay
signed in extends your client sign-in session to 30 days.

### Waivers, members and loyalty

We record the responsible person's details, declarations and signature, and
the details of participants they include on a waiver. Kiosk users can complete
a waiver without creating an account. At public events, scanning the loyalty
QR and selecting participants records a visit and awards points or redeems a
free paint. These records are per participant. Transfers of responsibility use
email approval and keep the participant's existing record and loyalty history.
Waiver validity is 12 months; this is not a promise that every associated record
is deleted after 12 months.

### Optional marketing and photographs

Marketing consent is optional and distinct from essential service messages.
Customers can unsubscribe through the link in marketing emails. We retain an
unsubscribe record to prevent further marketing. Photography/video permission
and giveaway entries are collected through the linked Cognito forms. The final
notice must explain actual use, publication destinations, withdrawal and how
permission is obtained for children; confirm these details before publication.

### Service providers

The app currently uses Render for hosting, Resend for email, Cloudinary for
images, Connecteam for staff rota integration, Cognito Forms for external forms
and SumUp for invoice/payment activity where used. Booking exports send event
references, times, venue and theme to Connecteam. Form entries stay in Cognito
and are not automatically imported. Confirm each provider's contractual role,
processing locations and any international transfer safeguards before stating
these as legal assurances. Distinguish linked Facebook/Instagram/Trustpilot
pages from providers that process app data for CL Paints.
