1. Booking eligibility

1.1 The person making the booking must be aged 18 or over.

1.2 If CL Paints reasonably believes that a booking has been made by a person under the age of 18, we reserve the right to request suitable evidence of age and may refuse to provide the service where the booking requirements have not been met.

1.3 The minimum age for a participant to receive face painting at a private event is 3 years old.

2. Booking information and attendance

2.1 The client is responsible for providing accurate information when making a booking, including the date, venue, times and a reasonable estimate of the number of people expected to attend.

2.2 The estimated attendance provided by the client is used by CL Paints to assess the requirements and suitability of the booking.

2.3 If the expected attendance changes significantly after the booking has been made, the client should inform CL Paints as soon as reasonably possible. We may recommend additional booking time where appropriate.

2.4 The number of attendees stated on the booking form does not represent a guarantee that every attendee will receive face painting.

2.5 CL Paints is booked to provide its service for the agreed period of time, rather than to complete a guaranteed number of face paints.

2.6 The number of participants who can be painted during the booking will vary according to factors including the designs requested, participant cooperation and reasonable operational circumstances.

3. Booking duration

3.1 CL Paints will provide face-painting services between the agreed start and finish times stated on the confirmed booking.

3.2 We will paint as many participants as reasonably possible during the agreed booking period.

3.3 The presence of participants still waiting to be painted at the agreed finish time does not automatically extend the booking.

3.4 Face painting will stop at the agreed booking finish time, regardless of whether participants remain waiting.

3.5 Clients should therefore consider their expected attendance when deciding how much face-painting time to book.

4. Additional time and extensions

4.1 If additional face-painting time is required, the client may ask CL Paints to extend the booking.

4.2 Additional time is entirely subject to availability. CL Paints is under no obligation to extend a booking beyond its agreed finish time.

4.3 Additional time is available in 15-minute increments and will be charged at the applicable additional-time rate advised to the client.

4.4 At the original agreed finish time, face painting will stop before any extension is discussed, confirmed or commenced.

4.5 Before additional time begins, the client must confirm the amount of additional time requested, agree to the additional charge, and make the required payment.

4.6 Face painting will only resume once the extension has been confirmed by CL Paints and payment has been received.

4.7 Once confirmed, the agreed additional time will be added to the booking and a new finish time will apply.

4.8 Purchasing additional time does not guarantee that every participant remaining in the queue will be painted.

4.9 CL Paints may refuse an extension where another booking, travel requirements, staff availability, venue requirements or other reasonable operational circumstances prevent us from remaining at the event.

5. Arrival and set-up

5.1 CL Paints may arrive at the venue up to one hour before the agreed start time to allow adequate time for set-up.

5.2 Reasonable set-up time before the agreed booking start time is not chargeable to the client.

5.3 The client should ensure that CL Paints has reasonable access to the venue and the agreed working area in sufficient time to set up.

6. Deposits and payment

6.1 A deposit of 50% of the total booking price is required to secure the booking.

6.2 The deposit will be requested using a payment link sent to the email address provided by the client.

6.3 Unless otherwise agreed in writing, any outstanding booking balance is due on the event date.

6.4 Accepted payment methods may include cash, card or bank transfer. Relevant payment information will be provided to the client.

6.5 Additional time purchased during an event is payable before the additional service begins, in accordance with Section 4.

6.6 Where an amount properly due remains unpaid, CL Paints reserves the right to take reasonable steps to recover the outstanding amount. This may include using an appropriate debt-recovery process where necessary.
