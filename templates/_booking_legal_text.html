1. Product & Safety Disclosure

CL Paints uses professional, cosmetic-grade, water-based, hypoallergenic face paints and cosmetic glitters intended for skin use. Products comply with applicable FDA (U.S.) and/or EU cosmetic safety regulations.

All reasonable hygiene and sanitation procedures are followed, including cleaning and sanitizing tools between participants.

However, cosmetic products may cause allergic reactions or skin irritation in rare cases.

2. Allergy & Patch Test Notice

Participants with sensitive skin, known cosmetic allergies, or those being painted for the first time are strongly encouraged to request a patch test prior to full application.

Patch tests are available upon request but do not guarantee that a reaction will not occur.

By choosing to proceed without a patch test, you voluntarily accept all associated risks.

3. Health & Contagious Conditions Policy

For health and safety reasons, CL Paints will NOT paint any individual with:

  • Open wounds or broken skin
  • Cold sores
  • Runny noses
  • Conjunctivitis (pink eye)
  • Rashes, eczema flare-ups, dermatitis, or visible skin irritation
  • Any contagious illness or infection

CL Paints reserves the right to refuse service for health, hygiene, or safety reasons.

4. Age Restriction

CL Paints does not paint children under 3 (0-2) years of age due to skin sensitivity and safety concerns.

A simple hand design may be offered at the artist's discretion as an alternative.

Age verification may be requested.

5. Supervision Requirement

A parent or legal guardian must be present and actively supervising minors at all times. (18+)

CL Paints does not provide childcare or supervision services.

CL Paints is not responsible for injuries resulting from a child's movement during or after application.

6. Removal Instructions

All face paint should be removed before bedtime using mild soap and warm water.

Avoid harsh scrubbing, baby wipes, alcohol, or abrasive cleansers.

Some pigments (especially reds, blues, and greens) may temporarily tint the skin but will fade naturally.

Failure to follow removal instructions may increase the risk of irritation. CL Paints is not responsible for issues resulting from improper removal.
