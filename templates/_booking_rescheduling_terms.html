19. Booking amendments and rescheduling

19.1 Requests to change a booking: Once a booking has been confirmed, the agreed event date, start time, duration and location form part of the booking agreement. Any request to change these details must be submitted to CL Paints in writing and is subject to availability and approval. Submitting an amendment request does not automatically change or cancel the original booking.

19.2 One free date change: All bookings include one free booking rearrangement. Customers may request this change of event date per confirmed booking provided at least 14 calendar days' notice is given before the existing confirmed event date. The requested new date must be available and agreed in writing by CL Paints. CL Paints may decline a requested date change where it cannot reasonably be accommodated.

19.3 Further date changes: After the free rearrangement has been used, each further approved rescheduling is subject to a £15 administration fee and requires at least 14 calendar days' notice before the existing confirmed event date. Further changes are considered at CL Paints' discretion and may be declined. The administration fee must be communicated and agreed before the amendment is confirmed. Where a further change cannot be agreed, the existing confirmed booking date remains in place unless the booking is cancelled in accordance with Section 8.

19.4 Changes within 14 days: Requests to reschedule within 14 calendar days of the confirmed event date are not automatically permitted. CL Paints will consider such requests individually, taking account of availability, staffing, travel arrangements, costs already incurred and the circumstances of the request. Where a change cannot be accommodated, the existing booking and the cancellation terms in Section 8 continue to apply.

19.5 Deposits and payments: Where CL Paints agrees to a new date, payments already made will normally be transferred to the amended booking, subject to any agreed changes in price or additional costs. A date change does not automatically entitle the customer to a refund. Any cancellation charges or retention of payments remain subject to the applicable cancellation terms and the customer's statutory rights.

19.6 Promotional and discounted bookings: Bookings made using a promotional discount, including Black Friday offers, are subject to the same amendment and rescheduling policy. An approved change of date does not automatically remove an agreed promotional discount. Any proposed change to the booking price must be communicated and agreed before the amendment is confirmed.

19.7 Exceptional circumstances: CL Paints may consider reasonable exceptions where serious illness, emergencies or other exceptional circumstances prevent the event from proceeding as originally arranged. Each request will be considered fairly and individually.

19.8 Confirmation of amendments: No amendment takes effect until CL Paints has confirmed the revised booking details in writing. Until written confirmation is provided, the original booking arrangements remain applicable. Nothing in this policy excludes or restricts a customer's statutory consumer rights.
