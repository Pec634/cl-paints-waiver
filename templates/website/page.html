A family business.

Established in 2025, CL Paints grew from Chloe’s love of art, makeup and creativity.

Qualified in makeup, hair and prosthetics, Chloe brings an artistic eye and a love of imaginative designs to every event. CL Paints takes its name from Chloe Lauren.

From colourful butterflies to playful characters, each design is a chance to make someone smile.
