Staff accounts & permissions

Create a named account, allocate a unique PIN and tick the areas that person can use. PINs have no minimum length and are never displayed after saving.

Permissions include viewing and managing records within the selected area. Till management also enables point of sale. Only your administrator account can change staff accounts, permissions, administrator settings and backups.

Changes apply on the staff member's next request. Existing till accounts keep their previous till access until you edit them.
