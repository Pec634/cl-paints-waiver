Help & aftercare

A little preparation helps your event run smoothly. For advice about your own event, contact us through the portal.

What should I check before my event?

Review your confirmed date, start and finish times, venue address and theme. Make sure your contact details are current. Let us know about access or setup arrangements, and check any deposit or balance due.

How do I know my booking is confirmed?

Your booking shows “Accepted” once CL Paints confirms it. “Under Review” means we are still checking your request. Open the booking for the latest status and arrangements.

Can I change my date, times or venue?

Open your booking and use “Request a change”. Include your proposed arrangements. Your booking stays as agreed until CL Paints approves the change. You can track the decision on your booking and dashboard.

Open my bookings
Where do I pay or find my invoice?

Your dashboard and booking show any attached SumUp invoices. Open an invoice to see its payment options. The portal balance is updated from payments recorded by CL Paints, so a recent payment may not appear immediately.

Do I need a waiver for a public event?

Complete a waiver before painting at a public event. You can review the members linked to your account under My waivers.

My waivers & members
What setup will you need?

Tell us whether your event is indoors or outdoors and share any venue access restrictions. Contact CL Paints to confirm the space, seating and setup needed for your particular booking.

Where can I find face-painting aftercare advice?

Ask your painter for removal and aftercare instructions for the products used at your event. If you need those instructions again, contact CL Paints with the event date and booking reference.

Can I turn off email updates?

You can choose optional booking and reward emails in Email preferences. Portal updates remain available, and verification codes and essential booking messages continue.
