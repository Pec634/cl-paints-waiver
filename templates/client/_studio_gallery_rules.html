Community gallery guidelines

CL Paints is a welcoming, family-friendly creative space. We do not condone content that promotes self-harm, abuse, hatred or harm to others. These guidelines apply to your design, its title and your display name.

Please do not submit:

  • Content that encourages, glorifies or gives instructions for self-harm, suicide, eating disorders or dangerous challenges.
  • Religious messaging, symbols or campaigning, or political campaigning. To keep the gallery focused on face painting and welcoming to everyone, we ask that these subjects stay out of shared designs.
  • Hate speech, discriminatory symbols, harassment, bullying, threats or extremist propaganda.
  • Nudity, sexual content, sexualised portrayals of children, profanity or obscene gestures.
  • Graphic injuries, realistic wounds, gore, or content celebrating real-world violence or abuse. Friendly fantasy characters and non-graphic Halloween designs are welcome.
  • Content promoting illegal drugs, smoking, gambling or other activities unsuitable for children.
  • Personal details such as full names, contact information, addresses, school names or account details; photographs of real customers or other people.
  • Advertising, scams, impersonation, or work you do not have permission to share.

Use a supplied studio face template or illustrated character. Every submission is reviewed before publication. CL Paints may reject or remove unsuitable content, including content not specifically listed here. Approval is not an endorsement of a creator’s views. You can report a published design or withdraw your own submission at any time.
