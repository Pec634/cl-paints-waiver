Refer a friend and earn 30 minutes of free face painting after their qualifying booking has taken place and CL Paints confirms attendance.

  1. Share your code

  2. Your friend books

    They enter the code exactly as shown on their booking request. Each code can be used once.

  3. Enjoy your thank-you

    After attendance is confirmed, your reward becomes available. Contact us to arrange how to use it.

Referral codes are valid for 12 months from the original attended event. Submitting a booking uses the code, even if that booking is later declined. Using a code does not immediately earn a reward. Once earned, your reward has no expiry and can be redeemed once.

Face-paint loyalty: three points, then a free fourth visit

At a public event, scan the CL Paints loyalty QR code, sign in and select only the waiver members being painted. Each selected person gets one point, once per event. No scan means no points. Private events do not count.

After collecting three points, that person’s next scanned public-event visit earns one free face paint of their choice from that event’s moodboard. Points belong to that person, cannot be shared, and reset after the free paint so they can collect again. A valid waiver is required.
