The headband itself is always black. Build three colour layers above it: the background backing behind the wording and shapes, the bold outline, and the letters. Choose solid plain letters or a glitter-paper letter colour. Every edit updates the live preview.

Every headband has two fixed spring coils. With two words, your first word sits on the left coil and your second on the right. With three words, the first and last sit on the coils and your middle word is suspended between them, joined by your selected shapes. The coils cannot be customised.

Choose a letter colour for each design, then pick plain or glitter letters for your finishing touch. Use “Add another headband” to mix colours, wording and letter finishes in the same order.

Make each headband your own with custom wording of at least two words and up to 16 characters, plus up to two spaces. Enter the quantity you would like for each design, and use “Add another headband” to include different wording or products in the same order.
