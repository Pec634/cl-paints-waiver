Create your headband in 3 steps

  1. Choose your wording

    Use one word with at least 7 characters, or two to three words. You can use up to 16 characters plus two spaces.

    Choose a shape between your words if you like. A shape is required for three words.

  2. Pick your colours

    The headband is black. Choose the background backing, bold border and plain or glitter finish for the design above it.

    Choose one overall colour, or give each word and each shape its own colour. The preview updates as you make changes.

  3. Set the quantity and order

    Enter how many you want. Use Add another headband for a different design in the same order.

    Fill in your delivery details and pay securely through SumUp. Your confirmation and order number are issued after payment is verified.

Need to pay later? Save the private link on the payment page, or return to the shop using the same browser. Unpaid orders are held for 3 working days (Monday–Friday), then automatically deleted.

How the wording sits on the headband

Every headband has two fixed springs. One word spans both springs. With two words, the first sits on the left and the second on the right. With three words, the first and last sit on the springs, and the shapes join the middle word between them.
