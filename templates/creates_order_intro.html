Order your custom headbands

Make each headband your own with custom wording of up to 11 characters, plus up to two spaces. Enter the quantity you would like for each design, and use “Add another headband” to include different wording or products in the same order.

Provide your first and last name and email, then choose UK mainland postage or free collection. For postage, enter your full delivery address and postcode.

We review your order and send a SumUp invoice. Your order progresses through under review, paid and in production, then shipped or ready for collection. We will send email updates when your status changes.
