Client photograph uploads

When you choose to share photographs, we record your verified account, responsible person's name, signature, consent wording, submission date and the names and identifying descriptions of the people shown. These records remain private. We review uploads before using approved images in our portfolio, website or social media. Embedded image metadata is removed from website copies.

Photo sharing is optional. You can withdraw permission in the client portal under My events → Upload photos or contact us. Withdrawal removes the photographs from this website and stops new use. Contact us about social media, printed copies, deletion requests or copies outside our control. We retain release records while needed to document authorised use and deal with privacy requests.

Website usage totals

We record daily totals of public website page views, referring website domains and approximate device types. Country codes may be included when supplied by our trusted hosting provider. These totals do not store IP addresses, visitor identifiers or full referring links, and do not use tracking cookies. They help us understand which pages visitors use.
