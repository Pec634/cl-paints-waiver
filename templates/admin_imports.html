Import clients & bookings

Upload a CSV or Excel XLSX file, match its columns and review the results before saving. Existing records are preserved.

Start with a template

Client CSV template Booking CSV template

Up to 500 rows and 5 MB per file. Use the first worksheet for Excel, with column headings in the first row. Dates: YYYY-MM-DD or DD/MM/YYYY. Times: HH:MM.

Booking imports use one event per row. Repeated days belong in separate records in this initial importer. Use an original source reference for repeat-file duplicate protection. Payment estimates do not count as money received.

What importing does

Imports add records without sending emails, taking payments or accepting consent on a client's behalf. Imported clients still need to verify their email to sign in. Duplicate clients are skipped, so existing profiles stay unchanged. Booking signatures and consent must be reviewed separately.

PDF, scanned documents and older XLS files are not supported in this version. Convert them to CSV or XLSX and check the extracted details first.
