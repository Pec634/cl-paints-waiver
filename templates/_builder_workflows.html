Booking tools and response limits

Use these controls for seasonal booking forms. Response limits and waiting lists also work for giveaways and consent forms.

Connected booking tools

Returning clients have prefilled details and can request changes, download calendar invitations and pay through SumUp in My bookings. Address suggestions use your configured Google Maps key.

Manage deposits, online checkout and invoices in Payments, and availability blocks in Calendar. Online checkout needs your SumUp API key and merchant code on the server. The existing reminder command can be scheduled by your hosting service; a saved form does not start a background scheduler.
