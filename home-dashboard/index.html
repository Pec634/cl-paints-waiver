Make yourself at home

Settings are saved in this browser on this device.

Find this in Google Calendar → Settings → your family calendar → Integrate calendar. Calendar display requires permission for the signed-in Google account and may be blocked by third-party cookie settings. You do not need to make it public.

Google’s calendar embedding guide ↗

This uses Google’s live calendar view. Create and edit events in Google Calendar; changes appear in the embedded view when it refreshes. No Google password is stored here.

Add a camera

Use a camera web page or your existing camera viewer for access. For inline video, use a browser-compatible MP4 or WebM stream from your local bridge. RTSP streams need a bridge such as go2rtc; browsers cannot play RTSP directly. Wansview Cloud support varies by model.

Use URLs without embedded usernames, passwords, or access tokens. Keep your camera viewer protected on your home network.
